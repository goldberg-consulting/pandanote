# Style guide

## Voice

Lead with the finding. Prefer concrete language and active verbs.
Keep each paragraph focused on one idea. Explain why a calculation matters.

## Evidence

Preserve equations, citations, and technical meaning. Label sample data clearly.
Flag missing evidence instead of inventing references.

## Revision brief

Clarify the argument while retaining the author's voice. Offer small, reviewable
changes. Separate observations from interpretations and future work.

Use this note as an **@** attachment when revising [[Field notes]].
